How The Receipts works

Choose a U.S. public company and ask about its history. We look up management statements and reported financial results, then compare them with the peers and dates you choose. Select a citation to check the evidence.

SEC filings supply the company records. FRED and historical share prices provide context. Coverage is limited to retrieved filings; revised economic data and adjusted prices are kept separate from what was knowable at the time.

The example cases include archived data for 2020–March 2025. Other companies and dates depend on live source availability.
